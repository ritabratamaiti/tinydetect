Private by design

Your content never
reaches our servers.

We don't run servers that see what you check. The detector is a file your browser downloads, like a font or an image.

  • No content uploads. Text and images are processed in the browser tab. The page downloads models, runtime files and Google Fonts.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for to use the web checker.
  • Offline checks in an open tab. Load the detector you need before disconnecting. Reopening the page still needs a connection.

FAQ

Questions

Is my text or image uploaded anywhere?

No. The detector runs inside your browser tab. Your text and images are read on your computer and are not sent to us or anyone else.

Why does the first check take a while?

The browser downloads the model files and prepares the detector. It caches those files for later visits, but still needs to load the model into memory when you reopen the page. Speed depends on your device. Offline checks work in an open tab after the detector you need has loaded; reopening the page needs a connection.

Is AI-generated text a bad thing?

No. AI can help with drafts, translation and editing. What matters is the quality of the work and whether its use is disclosed when the context calls for it. A detector can't judge accuracy, effort or intent, and a high score isn't proof of deception.

Can I rely on the result?

No AI detector is right every time. Very short text, heavily edited writing, screenshots and cropped images are harder to judge. Treat a high score as a reason to look closer or ask questions, not as proof, especially when someone's grade or job depends on it.

Which AI tools can it spot?

The text detector learned from writing by many chatbots, including ChatGPT, Llama and Gemma, and we test it on newer ones such as Claude. The image detector learned from pictures made by thousands of image generators, and we test it on Midjourney, DALL·E 3 and Flux.

Does it work on my phone?

It may, depending on your browser and available memory. The models can be demanding on phones; try a laptop or desktop if loading fails or checks are slow.

Can my school or company use it?

The public web checker is free to try. Self-hosting and reuse are subject to the code licence and separate model licences. Team installs, add-ons and a commercially licensed SDK are planned.
