Model files in this folder
=========================

Text detector: text_body.onnx, text_emb_int4.bin, text_emb_scale_f16.bin, text_bpe.json
  Derived from fakespot-ai/roberta-base-ai-text-detection-v1
  (https://huggingface.co/fakespot-ai/roberta-base-ai-text-detection-v1), itself fine-tuned from
  FacebookAI/roberta-base. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
  https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

  Changes made by Ritabrata Maiti (2026):
  - fine-tuned briefly on human and AI-written text, then averaged 50/50 with the original weights;
  - exported to ONNX with word embeddings split out and stored as int4 (one fp16 scale per 32 values);
  - transformer weights quantised to 4-bit and 8-bit blocks (MatMulNBits), 24 most sensitive matrices at 8-bit.
  The tokenizer vocabulary and merges (text_bpe.json) are unchanged from the original model.

Image detector: image_cf384_nb8.onnx
  Derived from OwensLab/commfor-model-384 (Community Forensics, https://github.com/JeongsooP/Community-Forensics).
  Changes made by Ritabrata Maiti (2026): exported to ONNX and weights quantised to 8-bit blocks (MatMulNBits).
